Split a Stripe invoice export by customer

Export invoice line items from Stripe as CSV, then upload or paste it. The file is processed in memory and is not stored. Amounts are summed as they appear in the CSV (negative amounts count as discounts), separately per currency.

Expected columns: a customer email column and an amount column. Optional: quantity, currency, invoice id, and metadata columns such as plan (metadata).
